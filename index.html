Playing on Xbox? Two steps, in this order:
  1. Press A on the button below to go fullscreen.
  2. Then hold the ☰ Menu button until Edge says Use game controls — that hands the controller to the game.

Set up your sail

Controller: D-pad to pick a setting, left / right to change it, ✕ to go sailing

How to sail her

An Optimist has one sail, one sailor, and the wind always comes from the top of the screen.

Controller

PlayStation layout — an Xbox pad is the same shape: ✕ ○ □ △ are A B X Y, Options is Menu, Create is View.
